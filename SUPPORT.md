# Support

- Technical questions and proposals: [PCCX Discussions](https://github.com/pccxai/pccx/discussions).
- Reproducible bugs: an issue in the relevant active repository; include the source SHA, tool versions, command and logs.
- First contribution: [Start here](https://github.com/pccxai/pccx/blob/main/START_HERE.md).
- Operations, rights and brand questions: [Transparency](https://pccx.ai/en/legal/transparency/) and contact@altifigence.com.
- Security reports: follow the affected repository's `SECURITY.md`, or the [organization policy](SECURITY.md). Do not publish sensitive reports in an issue.

PCCX does not sell subscriptions or process payments on pccx.ai.
Altifigence products and support have their own terms.
